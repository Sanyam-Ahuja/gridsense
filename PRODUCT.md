# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React + TypeScript + Vite; Node.js + TypeScript + Express; PostgreSQL for deployment. Demo uses synthetic meter readings.

## Users
University facility managers and energy analysts monitoring building consumption and investigating unusual loads.

## Product Purpose
GridSense turns validated meter readings into building-level load profiles, explainable anomaly signals, and practical investigation recommendations.

## Positioning
Power, accumulated energy, baseline, anomaly, and forecast stay visibly distinct and traceable to readings.

## Operating Context
Operators move between a campus building schematic, meter readings, historical load, and anomaly evidence to decide what to inspect.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The MVP covers a building and meter registry, synthetic telemetry ingestion, time-series summary, three baseline rules, anomaly acknowledgement, and evidence-backed recommendations. Campus coordinates, real utility tariffs, and real emissions factors are not provided; estimates must be labeled.

## Brand Commitments
GridSense; industrial energy intelligence, light neutral surfaces, slate typography, measured green/amber/red status colors, and charts with units as specified in prompt.md.

## Evidence on Hand
The reading schema, anomaly examples, workflows, and screen requirements in ../prompt.md. No real campus meter data is supplied.

## Product Principles
- Distinguish kW from kWh.
- Trace anomalies to readings and baselines.
- State assumptions behind estimates.
- Never treat an anomaly as proof of equipment failure.
